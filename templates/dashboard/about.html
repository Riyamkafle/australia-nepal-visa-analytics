Glossary of Terms For Non-Technical Readers
Lodged
The number of visa applications submitted, regardless of outcome.
Granted
The number of applications that were approved by the Department of Home Affairs.
Refused
The number of applications that were rejected.
Grant Rate
The percentage of applications that were approved. Calculated as (Granted ÷ Lodged) × 100.
Refusal Rate
The percentage of applications that were rejected. Calculated as 100 − Grant Rate.
Onshore
Applicant was already living in Australia (e.g. on another visa) when they applied.
Offshore
Applicant was living outside Australia (e.g. in Nepal) when they applied.
Sector
The type of education provider, e.g. Higher Education (university), VET (vocational training), or ELICOS (English language courses).
Financial Year (FY)
Australia's financial year runs from 1 July to 30 June. "2023-24" means 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2024.
3-Month Rolling Average
The average grant rate over the current month and the two months before it — smooths out month-to-month noise.
Forecast
A statistical projection of future values based on historical trends. Not a guarantee.
Confidence Band
The range (upper/lower bound) within which the actual future value is likely to fall.
